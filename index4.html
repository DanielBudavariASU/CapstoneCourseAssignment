What's the Point?!

Points are awarded to players in hockey based on their offensive contributions. A player receives one point for each goal scored and one point for each assist. Goals and assists are combined to calculate a player's total points, a key indicator of their overall offensive performance. There are two types of assists, but both are 1 point. There are primary and secondary, meaning the person who touched puck immediatly before the goal scorer's shot, a secondary assist is who had possesion before the primary passer.

There are 3 main archetypes of forwards and 3 artchetpes of defensemen. For forwards, there are: snipers - elite shooters and scorers who are best when the puck is on their stick for a shot, play makers - always look to pass first and look for creative plays, and two-way players - keep offense and defense in mind at all times, looking for the best offensive/defensive postion and play at any time on the ice.
For defensemen, there are offensive defensemen - elite shooting and passing ability, will join the rush and play good offense, defensive defensemen - the counter part of their O-men teammates, always looking to defend and use the body to slow opposition, and finally Two-way defensemen - very similar to forwards Two way players.
