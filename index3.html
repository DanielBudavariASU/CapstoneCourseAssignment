Boston Bruins

Founded in 1924, the Boston Bruins are one of the NHL’s Original Six teams with a rich history of success and a passionate fan base. Known for their tough, physical play style, the Bruins have fostered a "Big, Bad Bruins" reputation, emphasized by legendary players like Bobby Orr and Ray Bourque. The team’s defense-focused, aggressive approach continues today, backed by standout players like David Pastrnak and Brad Marchand, who bring skill and scoring power.

The Bruins remain a consistent playoff contender, showcasing a blend of strong defense, disciplined forechecking, and high-paced offense under head coach Jim Montgomery. In 2022-23 the Bruins set the all-time regular season win record with 65, smashing the previous top of 62.

Key Players: Boston Bruins

David Pastranak: RW

A high-scoring right winger for the Boston Bruins, David Pastrnak is known for his lethal shot, quick hands, and playmaking abilities. Nicknamed "Pasta," he consistently ranks among the league’s top goal scorers. Pastrnak’s offensive prowess and versatility make him a key player in Boston’s forward lineup, especially on the power play.

Brad Marchand: LW

Brad Marchand is a skilled and gritty left winger for the Boston Bruins, known for his goal-scoring ability, defensive awareness, and agitator style. He combines high hockey IQ with tenacity, excelling both at even strength and on special teams. Marchand’s leadership and intensity make him a consistent impact player. He is the Captain for the Bruins and embodies their game.

Charlie McAvoy: RD

As a premier defenseman for the Boston Bruins, Charlie McAvoy is known for his two-way play, physical presence, and strong skating. He excels in shutting down top opponents while contributing offensively, particularly in setting up plays. McAvoy’s poise and vision solidify him as Boston’s top defensive presence.

Jeremy Swayman: Goalie

Boston Bruins’ goaltender Jeremy Swayman is a young, promising player with a calm and composed style in the net. Known for quick reflexes and efficient positioning, Swayman’s adaptability and solid puck-stopping abilities have made him a valuable asset in Boston’s goaltending rotation, with potential for future stardom. His recent contract of 8x8.5 aav will keep him in Boston for quite a while.

Colorado Avalanche

The Colorado Avalanche, established in 1995 after relocating from Quebec, quickly found success, winning two Stanley Cups by 2001. Known for their fast, high-scoring, and skill-driven style, the Avalanche have thrived through stars like Joe Sakic, Peter Forsberg, and, today, Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar.

With a focus on speed and offensive creativity, they dominate through relentless puck movement and strong defensive support. Led by head coach Jared Bednar, the Avalanche continue to rank among the league’s elite teams, pursuing additional championships as a powerhouse in the Western Conference. They hope to get back to the final this year despite a very poor start of 0-4.

Key Players: Colorado

Cale Makar: RD

Cale Makar is an elite defenseman for the Colorado Avalanche, celebrated for his exceptional skating, puck-handling, and offensive instincts. His ability to control the game from the blue line makes him a top-tier playmaker and a consistent scoring threat. Makar’s high hockey IQ drives Colorado’s offensive and defensive plays.

Nathan Mackinnon: C

Nathan MacKinnon, a center for the Colorado Avalanche, is known for his explosive speed, powerful shot, and relentless drive. A premier player in the NHL, MacKinnon combines strength and finesse to dominate opponents and create scoring chances. His leadership and energy are central to Colorado’s high-powered offense. He was last years MVP with 143 points.

Martin Necas: RW

Necas is a dynamic winger for the Colorado Avalanche, blends size, skill, and scoring touch to dominate offensively. Known for his strong puck possession and ability to finish plays, Necas had a breakout year this season and hopes to carry that into the playoffs. His chemistry with Makinnon makes him a vital piece of Colorado’s success.

Mackenzie Blackwood: Goalie

Goaltender Blackwood is a steady presence for the Colorado Avalanche, known for his quick reflexes, flexibility, and strong technical skills. Col’s lack of dependable goaltending held them back at times, but now with Blackwood in net the Avs have bolstered their previous weakest link. Can he get it done this playoffs?

Dallas Stars

Founded in 1967 as the Minnesota North Stars and relocated to Dallas in 1993, the Stars captured their lone Stanley Cup in 1999 and have remained perennial contenders ever since. They’re known for a stout, two-way style anchored by elite defensemen like Miro Heiskanen and Esa Lindell, coupled with breakout offensive talents such as Wyatt Johnston.

Between the pipes, Jake Oettinger’s calm, athletic goaltending gives the team confidence in tight games. Under coach Pete DeBoer, Dallas marries structured zone coverage with quick transitions up ice, creating scoring chances off the rush. Their blend of young upstarts and veteran leadership keeps them in the Western Conference playoff mix year after year.

Key Players: Dallas Stars

Wyatt Johnston: C

Wyatt is 21, but is already becoming a STAR! He was top 3 in points for Dallas this season and already is top 3 in points for the Stars just 7 games in the playoffs. He recently signed an 8-year deal to stay with Dallas. Wyatt is a key piece in the future of the Stars and is one of the best young players in the league.

Mikko Rantanen: RW

Rantanen is one of the NHL’s premier goal-scoring playmakers, routinely posting 80–100 point seasons. His heavy shot and ability to find soft ice make him a constant threat in the offensive zone, while his size and reach allow him to protect the puck and drive the net effectively. His game 7 hat trick against the Avs this playoffs is one of the best stories in the nhl.

Miro Heiskanen: RD

Heiskanen blends elite skating with exceptional poise in both his own zone and on the rush. Since his NHL debut in 2017, he’s consistently ranked among the league’s top young blueliners, driving play up ice and contributing reliably on the power play. His combination of offensive vision and defensive awareness has made him a top defenseman.

Jake Ottinger: Goalie

Oettinger combines exceptional athleticism with a calm, technical style that belies his years. Since taking over the crease in 2020, he’s posted some of the highest save percentages among goalies facing heavy workloads, earning Vezina Trophy consideration. His quick reflexes and composure under pressure have made him the backbone of Dallas’s defense and a true rising star between the pipes.
