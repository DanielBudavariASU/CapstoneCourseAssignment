Biggest Story: Playoffs Have Begun!

The grueling jounrey for the Stanley Cup has begun! The best major league sport is hosting the best and most exciting playoffs for the BEST Trophy in sports. If a team wishes to make Lord Stanley their own - the must win 16 brutal games through 4 rounds of playoffs.

The Western Conference teams include:
Stars vs Avalanche, Jets vs Blues, Knights vs Wild, Kings vs Oilers.

The Eastern Conference teams include:
Leafs vs Senators, Panthers vs Lightning, Hurricanes vs Devils, Canadiens vs Capitals.

Last year's final culminated in the Florida Panthers narrowly edging out the Oilers in game 7 after the Panthers relinquished a 3-0 lead.

Players To Watch: Stanley Cup Playoffs

Nathan Mackinnon: Center

Nicknamed "Nate-Dawg", Mackinnon is one of the most talented and competitve players, ranking top 2 in the NHL in talent and points. He won the Hart (MVP) trophey last season, but personal stats don't matter to the DAWG - all he wants is to lift Lord Stanley again like he did in 2022.

Connor McDavid: Center

McDavid is easily the best player in the league and has an argument for being the most skilled player OAT'. Last year he led the playoffs with 42 points, winning the Conn Smyth even despite losing in the final, an impressive yet sad stat! McDavid has never won a Cup - maybe this is the year?

Alexander Barkov: Center

Barkov is one of the best 2-way forwards of the current era, winning the Selke Trophy (best defensive forward) for it last season. Although he is no Bergeron, Barkov is the most important player for the reigning cup champ panthers. Will he repeat this year and solidfy himself as a Finnish icon?

Connor Hellebuck: Goalie

"Helly" will win the Vezina trophy for the best Goalie this year, making it his 3rd total. He may even win MVP which would make him the first goalie in 10+ years to do so, but he struggles mightly in the playoffs - maybe pressure is too high? If the Jets hope to advance Helly needs to be himself.
