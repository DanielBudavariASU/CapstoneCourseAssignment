How Does it Work: Rules of Play


NHL hockey is played on an ice rink with two teams of six players each, including one goaltender, who aim to score goals by shooting a puck into the opponent's net. The game consists of three 20-minute periods, with the team having the most goals at the end winning. The game emphasizes speed, skill, and physicality, with regular line changes to keep players fresh.
